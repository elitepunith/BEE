📜 Manifesto

I am a beginner web developer on a journey to master the web, starting from the ground up. I believe in the power of the vanilla stack—HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—to build fast, lightweight, and completely custom experiences without relying on heavy frameworks.

To me, coding is like setting sail on a grand adventure. Whether I am building a simple interactive utility like my dependency-free browser roller Dice, or a 3D web experience, I want my work to embody absolute freedom, pushing past limits and experimenting with what the browser can do.

As elite_punith, I am committed to building things from scratch, participating in hack jams, and leaving my mark on the internet. The canvas is blank, the terminal is ready, and the journey has just begun.

👩🏽‍🚀 Projects

I focus on building clean, practical applications and engaging web experiences. Some of my recent work includes Unplug, a minimalist brutalist focus utility terminal, and Dentro, a gamified coding quiz designed to test JavaScript and Python knowledge. 💻 Tech Stack & Focus

Vanilla Front-End

I specialize in writing raw HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. By avoiding bulky external dependencies and build steps, I ensure my projects remain lightweight and maintain absolute control over the DOM.

3D & Animation

I experiment with Three.js to render interactive 3D environments in the browser. I also work with GSAP for responsive UI animations and write Python scripts for 3D automation and character animation inside Blender.

Game & App Development

From logic-based puzzle games to productivity tools like the cyberpunk-themed Orbit Focus Timer, I enjoy solving problems and creating interactive, user-focused digital tools from scratch.
